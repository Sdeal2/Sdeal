Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Smart Deal ("we", "us") runs the Smart Deal website and app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Order details you enter: your name, mobile number, delivery address, city, state, pincode, and the products, sizes and quantities you order.
  • Reseller details: if you place an order as a reseller, the related order and earnings information.
  • Basic usage information: an anonymous visit count (for example, whether you came from a search engine or a shared link), and items saved on your device such as cart, wishlist and name.

We do not collect your payment card, UPI or bank details. Orders are Cash on Delivery.

2. How we use your information

  • To receive, confirm, pack and deliver your orders.
  • To contact you about your order, including on WhatsApp or by phone.
  • To handle returns, support requests and reseller earnings.
  • To understand how many people visit and to improve the app.

3. WhatsApp

When you place an order, WhatsApp opens with your order details filled in. The message is sent only when you press send. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to messages sent through WhatsApp.

4. Where your data is stored

Order and account data is stored using Google Firebase (cloud database) and, for convenience, on your own device (browser or app storage). Clearing the app data on your device removes what is stored locally.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share your name, phone number and address only with delivery partners as needed to deliver your order, and with service providers that host our app (such as Google Firebase), or when required by law.

6. Data retention and your choices

We keep order information for as long as needed to deliver orders, handle returns and meet legal and accounting needs. You may ask us to correct or delete your personal information by contacting us below, and we will act on the request unless we are required by law to keep it.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no method of internet storage is completely secure.

8. Children

Our app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available on this page with its date.
